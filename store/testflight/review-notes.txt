TimeClaim records working hours and allowances and compares them with the user's payslip. The app is in German.

No account, no login and no server: all data is stored locally on the device. On iOS the app makes no network requests. No demo account is needed; on first start the app shows sample data ("Beispieldaten") that can be deleted.

How to test:
1. Tab "Tage": the days of the month. Tap "+" to add a working day with start, end and break.
2. Tab "Zulagen": the allowances of the month.
3. Tab "Abgleich": enter hours from a payslip and see the differences.
4. Gear icon (top right, "Einstellungen"): manage jobs and export or import a backup.
